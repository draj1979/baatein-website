Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Inferex Pvt Ltd (“Inferex”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit Smartleads websites or use the Smartleads platform and WhatsApp AI agent service (together, the “Service”). It applies to business customers, their team members, and people who contact us. A business customer is responsible for explaining its own privacy practices to people whose information it submits to the Service.

1. Who is responsible

Inferex Pvt Ltd operates Smartleads from Bhive, 27th Main, HSR Layout, Bengaluru 560102, India. For questions or privacy requests, contact us on WhatsApp at +91 86189 79775 or write to us at that address, marked “Privacy”.

2. Information we handle

  • Business and account details: business name and registration/KYC information, contact details, account credentials, team roles, and support communications.
  • Service content: documents, FAQs, instructions, configuration, WhatsApp messages, and related conversation records that a customer or its users connect or upload.
  • Usage and technical details: service activity, timestamps, device/browser and diagnostic data, and security logs needed to operate and protect the Service.
  • Commercial records: plan, usage, invoices, and payment status. WhatsApp conversation charges are handled by Meta through the customer's WhatsApp Business account. Payment information collected by a separate payment provider is governed by its notice.
  • Messages to us: information you choose to share when you contact us or request support.

3. How we use information

We use information to verify business accounts; provide, maintain, and secure the Service; retrieve relevant customer-provided material and generate AI replies; administer plans and usage; troubleshoot and support customers; prevent fraud and misuse; comply with legal obligations; and develop the Service using operational and diagnostic information. We do not use a customer's documents or conversations to advertise to that customer's contacts.

4. Customer content and roles

For documents, FAQs, and conversations a business customer submits, that customer decides why the information is used and is responsible for having an appropriate legal basis, providing required notices, and honoring requests from its own customers. Inferex processes that content to provide and protect the Service and follows the customer's documented instructions where applicable. For account administration, security, billing, and our direct communications, Inferex determines the purposes and means of processing.

Customers should only upload information they are authorized to use. Do not submit highly sensitive personal information unless your organization has confirmed that doing so is lawful and appropriate for the Service.

5. Service providers and disclosures

We share information with service providers that help us host and operate Smartleads, verify businesses, provide AI model capability, communicate through WhatsApp, process payments, and support security. The Service's primary application data is hosted in Google Cloud's Mumbai region. To generate a reply, relevant parts of a customer's documents and the conversation are sent to a third-party AI model provider; that provider may process the information outside India under its own service terms. WhatsApp and Meta process messages and account data under their own terms and privacy notices. Service providers handle information under the terms and safeguards that apply to our arrangements with them.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect people or the Service, to enforce our agreements, or as part of a corporate restructuring or transfer, subject to applicable law.

6. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service, maintain the customer's account, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the Service. Customer content is retained while the relevant account or feature is active and for a reasonable period afterward for deletion from backups and recovery systems, unless law requires longer retention. Exact deletion timing can depend on the service provider and backup cycle. We use safeguards intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

Account administrators can manage team access and customer content through the platform. You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis, or help with a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and authority before acting, and some records must be retained where required by law. If your information appears in a business customer's documents or conversations, please also contact that business directly; it controls that content.

8. Cookies and external services

This marketing website is a static site and does not currently include advertising or analytics trackers. The Smartleads platform and third-party services may use essential cookies or similar technologies for sign-in, security, and operation. Links to Meta, WhatsApp, and other sites are governed by those providers' own privacy notices.

9. Children and international processing

The Service is intended for registered businesses and their authorized adult representatives, not for children. The primary application data is hosted in India, but information sent to external providers, including AI and messaging providers, may be processed in other countries. Those transfers are subject to the applicable provider arrangements and law.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy as the Service or applicable law changes. We will post the current version here and update the date above. For questions or requests, contact Inferex Pvt Ltd at +91 86189 79775 on WhatsApp or at Bhive, 27th Main, HSR Layout, Bengaluru 560102, India.
