FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can sign up?

Registered businesses only. You complete business KYC when you sign up, and once you are verified your 7-day free trial begins.

What does the free trial include?

A full week to set up an agent with your own documents and connect your own WhatsApp number, so you can see real replies before you pay.

What counts as a reply?

A reply is a message your AI agent writes and sends to a customer. The Starter plan includes the AI charges for up to 1,000 replies per agent each month.

Do I pay WhatsApp separately?

Yes. WhatsApp's own conversation fees are charged by Meta to your WhatsApp Business account, so you need a payment method there. Smartleads's price covers the AI.

Which phone number can I use?

A number that is not already registered on WhatsApp or the WhatsApp Business app. You verify it during Meta's sign-up, which takes a few minutes.

What if the agent doesn't know the answer?

It tells the customer it doesn't have that information, instead of guessing. You can then add the missing document or FAQ.

Which languages does it reply in?

Agents reply in the language your customer writes in.

Where is my data stored?

In Google Cloud's Mumbai region, separated per business. To write a reply, the agent sends the relevant parts of your documents and the conversation to a third-party AI model provider.

Can a person take over a conversation?

Human hand-over is on our roadmap. Today your team can read every conversation in the inbox.
